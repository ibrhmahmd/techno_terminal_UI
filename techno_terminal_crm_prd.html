# Techno Terminal CRM — Frontend Handover & Product Specification **Audience:** Frontend Developers & UI/UX Designers **Goal:** Rebuild the UI from scratch in a modern framework. **Base URL:** `http://:8000/api/v1` **Auth:** `Authorization: Bearer ` ## Global Layout & Auth - **Sidebar Navigation:** Persistent, collapsible. - **Login Page:** Minimal form (Email, Password). - **Role-Based UI:** Admin/Manager (Full), Instructor (Dashboard/Directory RO), Receptionist (Directory/Enrollments/Finance). ## Core Pages & Priority 1. **Dashboard:** Daily operations "war room". 2. **Group Management:** Classes, sessions, attendance. 3. **Directory:** Students & Parents lookup/registration. 4. **Enrollments:** Student-group mapping. 5. **Finance:** Receipts and payments. 6. **Reports:** Analytics. 7. **Staff / HR:** Personnel management (Design target). 8. **Competitions:** Event management (Design target). ## Critical Components - **Attendance Grid:** Highly interactive session tracking with local state management. - **Edit Session Popup:** Modal for session-specific adjustments.
